How this log works (and its honest history)
  • Machine-readable source of truth: sla-compliance.jsonl — one JSON record per ticket. This page renders from it. Every record's hit/miss flags are recomputed from timestamps by tools/sla-validate.js; the summary below is produced by that same validator (sla-compliance-summary.json).
  • Measurement began 2026-09-23 under the v1.2 board. Zero tickets were ever filed in the v1.2 window — the log is never backfilled, so those rows stay blank honestly.
  • v2.0 targets took effect 2026-10-01; measurement against v2.0 begins that day. The first ticket received on/after 2026-10-01 appears here as row 1.
  • Only due tickets count against us: a ticket counts toward a rate only once its target deadline has passed — early answers are hits; not-yet-due is pending, never a miss.
  • Honest downgrade rule: 30 days with SLA missed on >20% of tickets → the published target is downgraded to what we actually achieved — dated, announced here, old target kept as history. Kill rule, never silent.
  • Owner-only topics (spending, wallet signing, identity, paid accounts) are excluded from timing: they queue to Black, and the queue time is shown, not hidden.
