How this log works (and how it started)
  • Measurement begins 2026-09-23. Day 1 of this log is 2026-09-23 — the day the SLA board v1.2 published named targets.
  • The log starts EMPTY and is never backfilled. No ticket is entered for a day when logging was not live. A blank row is an honest blank row, not a hidden miss.
  • Every ticket gets one row: received timestamp, unit (Care/Guide), tier (Free/Paid — everyone is Free until paid products launch), acknowledge time, substantive-response time, resolve/escalate time, and ✅/❌ against each target.
  • Honest downgrade rule: if any unit × tier misses its target on >20% of tickets over a 30-day window, the published target is downgraded to what we actually achieved — dated, announced in this log, old target kept as history. Kill rule: 30 days with >20% missing SLA → target honestly downgraded the same week.
  • Owner-only topics (spending, wallet signing, identity, paid accounts) are excluded from timing: they queue to Black, and the queue time is shown, not hidden.
